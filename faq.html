Frequently Asked Questions

Answers before you apply.

Everything developers, operators, and business owners ask us most about financing large-scale commercial projects with The Medley Group.

Q.What does The Medley Group do?

The Medley Group is a relationship-driven commercial loan brokerage firm. We connect developers, operators, and business owners with the right lenders to finance large-scale commercial real estate projects — structuring debt and equity packages from $10 million to $400 million and beyond.

Q.What size loans does The Medley Group arrange?

We specialize in large commercial transactions, typically ranging from $10 million to $400 million and above, structured to fit the scale and complexity of each project.

Q.What types of financing can you arrange?

We arrange construction financing, bridge loans, mezzanine debt, private (non-bank) debt, and equity placement — as well as acquisition, refinance, and recapitalization capital — structured to match the project and the sponsor's objectives.

Q.What types of projects and industries do you specialize in?

We focus on large-scale commercial developments including construction, hospitality, hotels, stadiums, healthcare, casinos, multifamily residential, and other major commercial real estate.

Q.How does the financing process work?

Our process has three stages: a Commercial Loan Consultation to evaluate your project, Loan Structuring & Packaging to present your application in the best possible light, and Financial Analysis & Strategy to guide your decisions. We then place your package with our lending partners and guide you through to term sheet and close.

Q.How quickly can I get a term sheet?

The Medley Group is known for fast term sheets. Exact timing depends on the complexity of the transaction and the completeness of your documentation, but our structuring expertise and lender relationships are built to move quickly.

Q.Do you work with non-bank or private lenders?

Yes. We maintain relationships with 50+ lending partners — including banks, private debt funds, and equity sources — so we can match each project to the most efficient source of capital.

Q.Who do you work with?

We work with developers, sponsors, operators, and business owners pursuing significant commercial real estate projects.

Q.What makes The Medley Group different?

A relationship-driven approach backed by more than 100 years of combined expertise, 50+ lending partners, and a 95% client success rate — all grounded in our values as a family-owned, faith-based firm.

Q.How do I get started?

Request a consultation through our contact form or email info@TheMedleyGroup.net. We'll review your project and map out the most efficient path to capital.
