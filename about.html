About The Medley Group

Lending built on relationships, not just numbers.

A relationship-driven commercial loan brokerage firm backed by more than 100 years of combined expertise — helping developers, operators, and business owners finance their most ambitious projects.

The Medley Group is a relationship-driven commercial loan brokerage firm. We connect developers, operators, and business owners with the right lenders to finance large-scale commercial real estate — structuring debt and equity packages from $10 million to $400 million and beyond. We treat every client as a partner, not a transaction.

Our team specializes in large-scale commercial lending across a wide range of industries. Whatever the scale or complexity, we bring the same disciplined approach, deep lender relationships, and genuine personal attention to every deal we take on.

As a family-owned firm grounded in Christian, faith-based values, we believe how you do business matters as much as the business you do. Integrity, trust, and real care for the people we serve guide every relationship we build.
