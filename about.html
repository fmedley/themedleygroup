About The Medley Group

Fred Medley

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Your vision. Our expertise. The capital to make it happen.

The Person Behind the Firm

Great projects deserve more than a financing proposal.

They deserve a financial partner who understands the vision, recognizes the opportunity, and knows how to bring the right people and capital to the table. That's why Fred Medley founded The Medley Group.

With a background spanning commercial banking, real estate development, financial services, and government leadership, Fred brings a perspective that few commercial financing advisors can offer. He understands both sides of the transaction — not only as an advisor arranging capital, but as a developer who has personally faced the challenges of securing financing, managing construction budgets, navigating regulatory requirements, and bringing projects to life.

He understands what's at stake because he's been there himself.

Through Medley Homes, his residential development company, Fred has renovated more than 48 properties and currently leads a planned $23 million residential development — overseeing everything from land acquisition and engineering to financing, permitting, and construction. That firsthand experience is what makes his approach different.

Expertise

Bringing the right capital to the right opportunity.

At The Medley Group, Fred specializes in arranging and structuring commercial real estate financing ranging from $10 million to $400 million and beyond. His expertise includes:

  • Commercial Real Estate Financing
  • Equity Financing & Strategic Equity Partnerships
  • Mezzanine Financing
  • Senior Debt & Private Debt Financing
  • Commercial Construction Financing
  • Bridge & Acquisition Financing
  • Capital Stack Structuring
  • Joint Venture Equity
  • Complex & Nontraditional Financing Solutions

His transaction experience includes a $40.5 million financing in Las Vegas, multifamily developments across Colorado, Missouri, and Florida, and large-scale infrastructure projects reaching into the hundreds of millions of dollars.

Through relationships with commercial banks, private lenders, debt funds, institutional investors, and equity capital partners, Fred works to identify financing solutions that align with each client's objectives, timeline, and long-term financial interests.

Whether you're developing a luxury hotel, acquiring a multifamily portfolio, building a mixed-use development, expanding a commercial enterprise, or seeking equity to complete your capital stack, The Medley Group approaches every opportunity with the same commitment: understanding your needs, identifying viable solutions, and working diligently toward a successful closing.

The Medley Group Difference

Relationships still matter.

In an industry increasingly driven by automated responses, transactional relationships, and impersonal service, Fred believes in doing business differently. He believes in answering the phone, returning calls, having candid conversations, and keeping clients informed throughout the financing process.

Sometimes the greatest value isn't simply finding a lender or an equity partner. It's recognizing gaps in a financing structure, identifying opportunities others may have overlooked, and bringing together the right relationships to move a project forward.

You're not just another transaction or a number in a pipeline. You're a business relationship worth investing in.

Leadership & Service

Leadership built on integrity, discipline, and service.

Fred's commitment to excellence began long before his career in finance. He proudly served in the United States Marine Corps as a member of the Presidential Honor Guard at the White House, serving during the administrations of Presidents Ronald Reagan and George H.W. Bush.

His professional career has included senior commercial banking leadership positions and distinguished public service, including serving as Director of the Indiana Department of Insurance and as a Commissioner on the Indiana Parole Board.

These experiences shaped his leadership philosophy: integrity is nonnegotiable, accountability matters, and your reputation is built on how you treat people and honor your commitments.

Beyond business, Fred has served in leadership, board, and treasurer roles for civic and nonprofit organizations, including Volunteers of America. Through Medley Innovation, he leads financial-literacy, entrepreneurship, and workforce-development initiatives designed to expand economic opportunity in underserved communities.
