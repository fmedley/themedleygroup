About The Medley Group

Lending built on relationships, not just numbers.

The Medley Group is a relationship-driven commercial loan brokerage firm backed by more than 100 years of combined expertise. We help developers, operators, and business owners secure the financing behind their most ambitious projects — and we treat every client as a partner, not a transaction.

Our team specializes in large-scale commercial lending across a wide range of industries, arranging financing for projects nationwide — in all 50 states and the Caribbean. Whatever the scale, complexity, or location, we bring the same disciplined approach, deep lender relationships, and genuine personal attention to every deal we take on.

As a family-owned firm grounded in Christian, faith-based values, we believe how you do business matters as much as the business you do. Integrity, trust, and real care for the people we serve guide every relationship we build.

Private Upscale Beach Club

$80MM Capital Stack

This project involved the financing of a private, upscale oceanfront beach club, conceived as a destination-level lifestyle asset combining exclusivity, hospitality, and wellness. Our team structured and delivered an $80 million debt and equity financing package to support a comprehensive amenity program — including an oceanfront pool, full-service spa and wellness center, and fine dining experiences designed to attract a high-net-worth, membership-driven clientele. Multifamily Residential with Retail

$54MM Capital Stack
